### Team Registration & VRS Invitation
This tournament features **no Direct VRS Invites** and **no Wildcard Invites**. All 4 Finals slots are earned through the Open Qualifier.
- Direct VRS Invites: 0 teams
- Wildcard Invites: 0 teams (with no VRS invitations, no Wildcard allowance exists)
- Open Qualifier: all slots
- Seeding: no VRS-based seeding is used. Under no circumstances will a roster without a Global VRS rank be seeded above a VRS-ranked roster.

### Registration Process
1. Register via the 5E Battle Platform under "Events – Tournaments" by selecting the relevant venue.
2. A live registration roster, including submission timestamps, will be published on a publicly viewable, read-only page (official 5E event page and official GitHub repository) so that registration time and order are publicly inspectable.
3. Registration order is determined by the platform submission timestamp, is publicly inspectable, and is not subject to manual adjustment.
4. Each team must register 5 starting players and up to 2 substitutes. All players must complete real-name verification, and their account identity must match their government-issued ID.
5. Maximum 32 teams per venue (identical for every venue). Registrations beyond the cap are waitlisted in timestamp order.
6. Registration deadline: 20:00 (Beijing Time) two days before each venue's start date.
7. Team eligibility documents must be submitted within 48 hours after registration; otherwise the venue slot is forfeited.
8. Registration applies no regional, ranking, or qualification-based filters: any team and player meeting the real-name verification and integrity requirements in this announcement may register.

### Open Qualifier
- Format: offline, at city internet-café venues. The final venue list and each venue's start date are published with this announcement and archived.
- Team Cap: 32 teams per venue (identical for every venue).
- Format: single elimination; the venue final is BO3, all other matches are BO1 (identical for every venue; venues may not modify the format).
- Advancement: the champion of each venue advances to the Online Play-In Stage (1 slot per venue, identical for every venue).
- Prize Money (identical for the same placement at every venue, pre-tax): Champion 2,000 CNY; Runner-Up 1,000 CNY.
- Map Pool: updated in sync with Valve's official competitive map pool — currently Mirage, Dust 2, Ancient, Inferno, Nuke, Anubis, Cache.
- Map Ban/Pick and Side Selection:
  - BO1: Team A ban, Team B ban, Team A ban, Team B ban, Team A ban, Team B ban; the remaining map is auto-picked; knife round decides sides.
  - BO3: Team A ban, Team B ban, Team A pick, Team B side, Team B pick, Team A side, Team A ban, Team B ban; the remaining map is auto-picked; knife round decides sides.
- Timeouts: 3 tactical timeouts (30 seconds) per team per match, regardless of half; technical timeouts and restores follow the official rulebook.
- Lateness & Roster Size: a team not fully present within 15 minutes of the scheduled start forfeits the match; a team unable to field at least 3 registered players for the entirety of a match forfeits that match.
- Match Environment: all matches are played on the 5E Battle Platform. Only players and authorized organizer staff may be in the server. Agent selection is unrestricted; referees may require default agents in specific circumstances.

### Themed Venues
Selected venues (provisionally 4) host brand and audience-focused on-site activities: partner pop-ups, on-site engagement, broadcast and promotional support.
- Competitive parameters are **identical** to non-themed venues: team cap, format, map pool, ban/pick, prize amounts, and advancement slots are all the same.
- **No additional prize money and no additional advancement slots.**
- Streamer activity is a separate exhibition segment: streamers are not participants of this tournament, do not count toward any match result, do not occupy advancement slots, and do not share in prize money.
- The operator provides no appearance fees or other additional compensation to any participating team. Themed-venue and non-themed-venue participants are subject to exactly the same rules and standards.

### Online Play-In Stage
- Format: online.
- Participants: champions of all Open Qualifier venues (approximately 50 teams).
- Groups: a publicly streamed draw splits the teams into 4 groups of as even a size as possible; **no regional division is used**.
- Format: single-elimination, BO3, within each group; the champion of each group (4 teams total) advances to the Finals.
- Brackets: determined by a publicly streamed draw; no VRS-based seeding.
- Prize Money (identical for the same placement in all 4 groups, pre-tax): Group Champion 3,500 CNY; Group Runner-Up 2,500 CNY.
- Non-cash Awards: Group Champions and Group Runner-Ups receive the "5ECC Online Play-In Group Champion / Runner-Up Certification" (identical standard across all 4 groups).
- Stage Status: this stage is played entirely online.

### Finals (Main Stage)
- Format: offline (LAN) in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province.
- Participants: the champions of the 4 Online Play-In groups (4 teams).
- Format: double elimination; the first round is BO1, all other matches are BO3.
- Brackets & Seeding: determined by a public draw; no VRS-based seeding.
- Prize Money (pre-tax): Champion 20,000 CNY; Runner-Up 10,000 CNY; 3rd Place 6,000 CNY; 4th Place 4,000 CNY.
- Non-cash Awards: the Champion and Runner-Up earn qualification for the 5E Professional Challenge (the terms of that qualification are governed by the Professional Challenge's own rules); the Champion, Runner-Up, 3rd Place and 4th Place each receive the corresponding 5ECC certification and avatar frame; all participating players receive the 5ECC participation avatar frame.
- Equal Treatment: all 4 participating teams receive identical standards for venue, equipment, practice time, technical support, and rule enforcement.

### Fairness & Eligibility Regulations
**Disqualification & Ban Policy (applied uniformly to all participants)**
Participation will be rejected or revoked in the following circumstances:
- Players with an active VAC ban issued within the 5 years preceding the Eligibility Reference Date (September 24, 2026)
- Players who received a VAC ban after participating in any Valve VRS circuit tournament (including qualifiers)
- Players or teams currently suspended by ESIC or with verified match-fixing records
- Players or teams with permanent bans from third-party platforms for integrity violations
- Use of cheat programs, account sharing/ghosting, identity fraud, or match manipulation

**Eligibility for Entry**
This tournament applies no regional, ranking, or qualification-based entry restrictions. Any team and player who completes real-name verification and does not fall under the Disqualification & Ban Policy above may register. Any individual disqualification decision will be published immediately at the time it is made.

**Roster Rules**
- Each team registers 5 starting players and up to 2 substitutes; rosters are frozen after each stage's Roster Lock Date.
- A team unable to field at least 3 registered players for the entirety of a match forfeits that match.
- During the event, each player may represent only one team.
- Teams that have advanced to the Online Play-In Stage may not re-enter other Open Qualifier venues of this tournament (an intra-tournament duplicate-entry restriction).

**Non-Exclusivity**
The tournament operator does not engage in any direct or indirect agreements that restrict teams, rosters, or players from taking part in other tournaments. This tournament only restricts duplicate entry within this same tournament; it does not restrict participation in any other tournament.

**Non-Discriminatory Treatment**
All participants at the same stage receive equal treatment in all aspects, including accommodation (not applicable to online stages), allowances, venue access, practice conditions, preparation time, technical support, and rule enforcement.

**Conflict of Interest Disclosure**
The tournament operator and event staff have no business relationships with any participating team, roster, or player, including shared management, shared ownership, licensing, or loans (no Entanglements). Streamer activity at Themed Venues is exhibition-only and involves no participant compensation. Should any business cooperation arise, it will be publicly disclosed before the event and disclosed in writing to Valve pursuant to Section 5.3 of Valve's rules.

**Scope of Referee Discretion**
Referees have discretion over in-match technical and rule infractions (warnings, round penalties, map forfeits, single-match forfeits). Decisions concerning participation eligibility and tournament disqualification are made exclusively under the objective criteria published in this announcement, and the details of any such decision are published immediately.
